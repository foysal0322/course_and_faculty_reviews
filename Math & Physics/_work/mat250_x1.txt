You should take multiple CSE courses early. The earlier courses (100, 200 and some 300 level courses) are relatively easy.
You will eventually have to take multiple CSE courses together in your final year, but final year courses are much more difficult to manage. I wish someone had told me this when I started at NSU. In your final year, almost all CSE courses will have projects, and the course content will be much harder to understand.
So, in your earlier semesters, take multiple CSE courses together. The 100 and 200 level courses are very easy.
