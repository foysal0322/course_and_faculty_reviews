[11.7]|Marjan Alam Unnoti 3 weeks ago|Faculty Review
if you want “easy-going” faculty, avoid him. if you want a professor who knows what he's doing and gives deserving grades, go ahead.
★ TEACHING:
he teaches very well (imo), explains in a clear way with examples. his slides are enough for most chapters but he'll tell you to read the book for some specific chapters and if you want good grades you should. take notes in class, draw all the graphs and practice the sums. he appreciates students who put genuine effort in class.
★ GRADING:
extremely strict/rigid (he'll say it himself on the introductory class and he's a man of his words). but he's very fair and doesn't decurve or curve at all. you'll get exactly what you earned by your own efforts. no less, no more.
he likes detailed answers for mids and finals, your answer needs to have all relevant information, multiple graphs wherever applicable and examples. however, detailed answers doesn't mean adding irrelevant information. his question pattern is very straightforward but he puts pressure through time. he takes 2 mids and both are counted.
for quizzes, it's objective questions mostly. Fill in the blanks, maths, sometimes graphs. best 2/3 quizzes taken.
he gives full marks for attendance if you have 70% attendance. while he's lenient about attendance he expects punctuality to be maintained on the days you're present.
lenient about make-up exams. no presentations or assignment.
★ EXTRA:
he's very helpful and encourages students to visit during office hours if they need any help. he'll give lots of suggestions, tips and question examples before exams and tell you some of the important topics beforehand. he invigilates very strictly during exams.
and while many students see "rigid" as a rather negative trait, i believe that faculties who give deserving grades, teach well and give proper guidance for exams are one of the best in the field of academics.
